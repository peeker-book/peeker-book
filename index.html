♦ About the Authors

Eshaan Gubbi and Ishaan Vora are freshmen (class of 2030) at Olentangy Liberty High School in the Olentangy Local School District. When they're not lost in a book- reading one or writing one, they're usually on a tennis court, chasing the same competitive edge that drives their fiction. Peeker is their debut thriller, built from a shared love of storytelling and suspense. Now, as first-time authors, these two young writers are ready to hand their story of high-stakes cons and closer-than-you-think loyalties over to the world.
